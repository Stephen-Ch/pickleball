Pickleball Rules

Basic Rules:

  • Underhand Serve: Must be served underhand below the waist
  • Double Bounce Rule: Ball must bounce once on each side before volleys
  • Non-Volley Zone: 7-foot zone on each side of net where you can't volley
  • Scoring: Only serving team can score points
  • Win Condition: First to 11 points, must win by 2

Court Layout:

  • 20' x 44' court divided by a net
  • Non-volley zones (kitchen) are 7' from the net
  • Service courts are behind the non-volley zones
